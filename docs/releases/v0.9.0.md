# HomeTiles v0.9.0

v0.9.0 connects panels to the Bridge directly without MQTT and adds head bar layouts with pages, an upright mode, a light theme, and a new Settings screen with a first-start setup on the panel.

## Highlights

- Direct link to the Bridge: press **Pair** on the panel and add it in Home Assistant. No MQTT broker is needed; existing MQTT setups keep working.
- Layouts: the new head bar layout shows fewer, larger tiles under a head with title, time and gear. ESP32-P4 panels mounted upright get their own upright layout. The Web Admin's layout window keeps separate places for every layout.
- Pages: up to five folders become pages beside Home. Dots in the head show the page; swipe or tap beside the dots to turn it, or open the overview.
- Light theme: Dark, Light or Automatic (light by day, dark by night), on the panel and in the Web Admin.
- New Settings on the panel with Wi-Fi list, keyboard with accents and French AZERTY, and a first-start setup in four steps.
- Entity picker like in Home Assistant: search every entity through the Bridge, with Recent and Favorites on top (#79).
- Clock tile: analog and wall clock styles, weekday and more date formats, rolling digits (#13). The Text tile has nine alignments, half height and a transparent style.
- Cameras: full screen on every ESP32-P4 panel at up to 30 FPS (#65), camera streams on the ESP32-S3 panels, camera tiles that show the picture (#50), and the screensaver can show a Home Assistant camera or image (#69). A View select option opens a camera straight in full screen, for example for a doorbell.
- Simpler colors: a tile's color always comes from its icon, with one **Fill** option; color rules can fill a tile too.
- Edge swipes for Settings, Home, Back and the screensaver.
- The Weather tile can show its own temperature and humidity sensors, in the weather entity's unit (#85, #86 by @derhally).
- Faster popups, folders and screensaver, and many fixes, including the Safari selection ring (#80), gauge decimals (#83) and the view catalog language (#72).

## Update Notes

Update to **[HomeTiles Bridge v0.9.0](https://github.com/GalusPeres/HomeTiles-Bridge/releases/tag/v0.9.0)** first and export your dashboard in Web Admin under **Import / Export** before updating.

Use the regular `.bin` for OTA or the browser installer's **Update** mode. Use `_factory.bin` only for a first installation or an intentional reset. Select the exact board and revision.

Going back to v0.8.0: v0.8.0 only knows the classic layout. Before downgrading, open the layout window, switch to **Classic**, make sure no tile is red, and save. Panels mounted upright show the classic landscape layout on v0.8.0. v0.8.0 needs MQTT; a panel on the direct link must be added in Home Assistant over MQTT again. Updating to v0.9.0 again restores every layout, page and place. See [Going back to v0.8.0](https://galusperes.github.io/updating/#going-back-to-v080).

## Hardware Confirmed

The maintainer tested this release on M5Stack Tab5, Waveshare LCD-4B and LCD-8, Guition JC8012P4A1 V2 and Guition ESP32-4848S040 (S3), including the direct link, head bar layouts, pages and the light theme. Built-in cameras were tested on Guition V2 and Tab5. A downgrade to v0.8.0 and the update back were tested on the Waveshare LCD-8.
